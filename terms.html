Agreeing to these terms

What Qpio is

Qpio is a curiosity quiz: five questions a day, other games, Qpio Gym, videos, pictures, and links to books, places and sources.

  • Free. Qpio is free to use, and there is nothing to buy in it today. If Qpio ever charges for something, it will be for optional extras. The questions, the explanations and the sources stay free, and you will always see a price before you pay anything.
  • No account. Your progress, your settings and anything you save are kept in this browser, on this device. Qpio cannot see them and cannot get them back for you. To move your progress to another device, use your backup code, in Settings.
  • Still being built. We may add, change or remove features, for example to improve the questions, fix a problem, keep Qpio secure or follow the law. If we ever stop running Qpio, we will say so in the app in good time, so you can save a copy of what is on your device.

The questions

  • Questions are written and checked against published sources. Answers show a source you can open and check yourself.
  • Mistakes can still happen. Facts about places, such as opening times, prices and rules, can change: check with the place before you go.
  • Qpio is for learning and fun. It is not professional advice of any kind, whether medical, legal, financial, travel or anything else. Do not rely on it where a mistake could harm you or cost you money.
  • If you think something is wrong, we want to know. The way to tell us will appear under “Who runs Qpio” once Qpio has an address.

Qpio Gym

Qpio Gym has daily puzzles, short moves for your hands and fingers, and small everyday things to do a different way, such as crossing your arms the other way round or tapping your foot while you count. The moves are meant to be done sitting down.

  • Only do what feels comfortable. Go gently, and stop if anything hurts.
  • If you have an injury or a condition that affects how you move, for example your hands, wrists, arms, shoulders, legs or feet, ask a doctor before doing the moves.
  • Do the moves sitting down, never while walking, cycling or driving.
  • Younger children should have a grown-up nearby.
  • Qpio Gym is for fun and practice. It is not a treatment, a therapy or medical advice.

Pictures. Most question pictures come from Wikimedia Commons. Each one stays under its own licence, chosen by the person who made it, and does not belong to Qpio. Some illustrations are generated by AI, and they are labelled that way.

Links. Qpio links to books, places, museums, sources and other websites. Other people run them, under their own terms and privacy rules, and Qpio does not control them. A link does not mean they support Qpio, or that Qpio agrees with everything they say. Qpio earns nothing from its links today. If a link ever earns Qpio money, it will say so next to the link, and that will never change which links you are shown.
