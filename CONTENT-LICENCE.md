## Where our facts come from

Every question carries a public source link, and we add more over time. **Facts
themselves belong to nobody** — the year of a battle, the height of a mountain,
the name of a discoverer. What is ours is the way we choose, write, verify and
arrange them.

Where a source link points to Wikipedia, that article's own licence
([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)) governs that
article — not our text.
